**1973:** Bruce and his unnamed band play the Bristol Motor Inn in Bristol, Rhode Island.

**1974:** Bruce goes to New York City to visit WPLJ-FM, where he sits down for an interview with Father Bill Ayres. They talk for over an hour, but the interview won’t be aired until April, and even then only in significantly edited form.

**1978:** Bruce completes his west coast Darkness swing with a show at the Seattle Center Arena, where he and the E Street Band play “Pretty Flamingo” for the first time since the Born to Run Tour. Well after the show ends, after most fans have filed out of the arena and the house lights are on, Bruce returns to the stage to perform two more songs–“Rave On” and “Twist and Shout.”
