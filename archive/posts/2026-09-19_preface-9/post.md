And so we’ve reached the end of the *Cover Me* series.

It’s incomplete, as it was always destined to be. Trying to catalog and chronicle every song Bruce Springsteen has ever covered is the very definition of chasing a moving target.

Even so, I’m sure some of you will notice the absence of a few classics. I’m at peace with it, though. John Fogerty has so many entries in this series that I can forgive myself for never getting around to “Proud Mary.” (I did write an entry for “Travelin’ Band” specifically for this book, though. That one I couldn’t leave out.)

There is, however, one particular cover that will probably render you aghast if and when you realize its omission.

About that I will only say this: I left it out on purpose, because from my very first day as a blogger, I knew it would be the last essay I would ever write about Bruce Springsteen. It’ll see publication as the last entry in Volume 50 of the Bookshelf Collection sometime in late 2027 or early 2028.

For now, thanks for reading my *Cover Me* posts over the years, even though I know many of you were hoping each day for a *Roll of the Dice* entry instead.

> *Cover Me* was the series I always enjoyed writing the most. I hope you enjoy reading final collection.
