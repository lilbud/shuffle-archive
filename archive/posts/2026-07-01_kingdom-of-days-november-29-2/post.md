**1977:** At the Record Plant in New York City, Bruce records “The Brokenhearted.” He won’t release it for another 33 years.

[Watch on Youtube: Watch Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YvilYiGDmoM)

**1983:** Bruce holds a recording session for *Born in the U.S.A. at* The Hit Factory in New York City.

**1984:** Bruce and the E Street Band play their first of two Born in the U.S.A. shows at The Summit in Houston, where “Factory” gets its last outing for fifteen years.

[Watch Video Highlight](https://videopress.com/embed/7IdXR5EB)

**1986:** Bruce debuts the video for his new single, “War,” from his *Live 1975-85* box set.

[Watch on Youtube: Watch Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mn91L9goKfQ)

**1995:** In a departure from tour (this or pretty much any other) precedent, Bruce has an opening act at his solo acoustic show at Berkeley Community Theater. John Wesley Harding becomes the first opening act for Bruce in twenty years.

[Watch on Youtube: Watch Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lKcWzWPHVj0)
