If your band’s genre is a mix of Irish traditional and americana music, sooner or later you’re bound to cover our greatest (half-)Irish americana artist. Solas chose a great Springsteen original to cover–their take on “The Ghost of Tom Joad” captures its spirit in an (appropriately) haunting arrangement.
